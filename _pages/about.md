My name is Xiaohang Sun. I am a PhD student in Political Science at Penn State. I study elections and civil conflicts in developing countries. My recent projects focus on the elections and the Maoist insurgency in India and the distributive consequences of electoral politics in Cambodia. Before joining Penn State, I earned dual bachelor’s degrees in international politics and economics from Shandong University, China, in 2024. I then pursued a master’s degree in international relations at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.
